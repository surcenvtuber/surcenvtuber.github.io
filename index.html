Start a commission

Six steps from first message to finished files.

What I need from you

  • A character reference sheet as a PSD with separate layers — the same thing Live2D modelling asks for.
  • If this is an update to an existing model, the .vroid file of the previous one, so the body measurements carry over.
  • The full payment before I start.
  • Confirmation that you've read and agree with the terms above.

  1. Check the queue and send a DM

    Look at the waiting list first, then message me on Twitter or in my Booth inbox with your reference sheet, front and back. The reference sheet is required.

  2. I reply with the details

    Price for your specific commission, expected start and finish dates, and anything else worth settling early.

  3. You join the waiting list

    If the terms, price and timing work for you, I add you to the list — there's no fee for that. I contact one person at a time in queue order. If you've changed your mind by the time I reach you, I move to the next person, no hard feelings.

  4. You pay in full

    When I get back to you, the full payment goes through PayPal in USD.

  5. You see three previews

    I send a preview when the body textures are done, when the hair is done, and when the clothes are done. At each of those points you can ask for small changes at no extra cost.

  6. I deliver the files

    Files come to you through Google Drive, and I post the result on my social media, tagging your Twitter unless you tell me not to. Private commissions cost 50% more and never get posted anywhere.
